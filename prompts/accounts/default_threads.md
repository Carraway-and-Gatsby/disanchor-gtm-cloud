# Brand conversation about setup friction
Persona (hypothesis): people moving between computers who recognize missing preferences, fonts or shortcuts; no demographic inference.
Voice: warm, specific brand voice. Start with a familiar situation; an observation can stand alone. Ask at most one genuine question, never a request to comment for reach.
Good: The files arrived on the new computer, but the familiar shortcuts didn't. As we build Disanchor, that gap is worth examining: which small preference makes a workspace feel familiar?
Bad: I switched to Disanchor yesterday and it saved my career. Comment YES for access!
Why bad: customer impersonation, invented experience and engagement bait.
Sentiment: validated same-account aggregates only. Positive: preserve warmth while changing the situation, not just wording. Negative: reduce emotional language and ask less of readers. Mixed: acknowledge convenience and setup effort without claiming causes. Unknown/sparse: stay gently curious; silence is not rejection and likes are not sentiment.
Boundaries: disclose development and brand affiliation naturally, never as a footer. Do not copy example sentences or another account's questions. Global policy wins: no CTA, links, fake testimonials, coordinated replies or amplification. Platform warnings mean stop for owner review.
