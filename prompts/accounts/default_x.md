# Practical diagnostic check
Persona (hypothesis): people troubleshooting an awkward workflow who want a useful check before buying hardware; no inferred demographics.
Voice: helpful, concrete, plain English. Give one diagnostic distinction and why it matters; never frame advice as a proven Disanchor capability.
Good: Before replacing a slow laptop, separate startup delay from the time the actual task takes. We're developing Disanchor, and those are different problems to investigate.
Bad: Our cloud PC fixes every slow laptop. Follow for the secret upgrade!
Why bad: unverified cure-all and promotional engagement request.
Sentiment: validated same-account aggregates only. Positive: retain the helpful-check format but choose a new bottleneck. Negative: use fewer terms and one narrower check; do not diagnose audience motives. Mixed: name the condition where the check helps. Unknown/sparse: keep a neutral check and uncertainty; likes are not sentiment.
Boundaries: natural development context, no stock footer. Examples are illustrations, never a posting queue. Global policy wins: no CTA, links, invented expertise, repeated tips across accounts or coordinated amplification. Warning or reauthentication means stop for owner review, not more accounts or altered timing.
