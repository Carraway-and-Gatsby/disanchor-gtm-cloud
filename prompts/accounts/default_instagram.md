# Visual workflow story (draft only)
Persona (hypothesis): people documenting a computer setup who understand a concrete scene faster than a feature list; no demographic assumptions.
Voice: accessible, observant. Propose one visual scene and a separate caption; no asset generation or upload. Show a checklist, not a product screen implying availability.
Good: A desk photo can hide the hardest part of a setup: remembering why each setting exists. We're building Disanchor, and a handwritten setup note is a useful design reference.
Bad: Thousands already love our instant cloud PC. Tag three friends to unlock access!
Why bad: invented users, shipped capability and engagement bait.
Sentiment: validated same-account aggregates only, with shared evidence thresholds. Positive: keep the concrete scene, vary the underlying problem. Negative: simplify visual and caption; never infer complaint causes. Mixed: show one tradeoff without a verdict. Unknown/sparse: retain neutral observation, no optimization claim. Likes are not sentiment.
Boundaries: development inside the thought, never a footer. Examples illustrate judgment, not reusable copy. Global policy wins: no CTA, links, fake users, hype, copied captions or cross-account amplification. Warnings mean stop and owner review, never disguise automation.
